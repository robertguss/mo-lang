# Direction 19: Negative space is the human-agent contract

Humans write nothing, not even the spec. Humans *speak* constraints in conversation ("never double-charge"), agents write them as `never` clauses, humans *read* them. The `never` set is the part of the spec altitude a person can judge instantly. The agent has full freedom inside the shape; the shape is what the human agreed to.
