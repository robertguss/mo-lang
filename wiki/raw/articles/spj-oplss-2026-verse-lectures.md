# The Verse Language: Types, Semantics, and Verification 

# Abstract

This is a series of four tutorial-style lectures, given at the Oregon Programming Languages Summer School 2026. The cover the programmer’s-eye view of Verse, its semantics (both operational and denotational), and how to verify (i.e. typecheck) Verse programs.

These lectures describe work in progress!
